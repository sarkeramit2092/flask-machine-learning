DRY 
- Don't Repeat Yourself!!

- We have home and about html files where we are repeting our code!!
- So, use a layout.html (common bluprint of every page) and inherita the file from other html could be a smarter solution.

- We will incorporate that layout.html page code inside our home.html file. And how we gonna do it?

home.html
  {% extends "layout.html" %}

explain:
--------

- {} means jinja code.
- And we are basically saying that home.html inherits from layout.html!!

=======================================================
** What is happening?
- We came to the home endpoint; we called this home.html with the perameter (title = "Home") home.
- And in home.html, we told that home.html is actually inheriting from layout.html. So, the perameter that will be propagated to layout.html file. This called inherit.
